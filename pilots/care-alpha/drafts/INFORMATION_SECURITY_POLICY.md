# Draft Information Security Policy — Synthetic Care Alpha

Status: `DRAFT / NOT APPROVED / DOES NOT PROVE IMPLEMENTATION`

## Purpose

Protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information needed to provide safe care and operate the organization.

## Principles

- management owns information-security risk and reviews material status periodically;
- access is limited to legitimate business/care need and protected proportionately to risk;
- critical information, systems and suppliers are inventoried and assigned owners;
- security incidents are reported quickly and handled through defined escalation;
- recovery of critical information/services is tested rather than assumed;
- suppliers are governed according to security, privacy and continuity criticality;
- personnel receive practical, role-appropriate security/privacy awareness;
- controls are periodically checked for effectiveness and findings are tracked to closure.

## Responsibilities

The managing director is accountable for the management system. Operational ICT may be outsourced, but accountability for risk, requirements and evidence remains with the organization. Control owners maintain implementation and evidence; a qualified reviewer challenges material conclusions where required.

## Review

At least annually and after material organizational, technology, incident or regulatory change. Approval/communication records are required before this draft can be treated as an implemented policy.
