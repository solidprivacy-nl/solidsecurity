# Draft Security & Privacy Incident Response — Synthetic Care Alpha

Status: `DRAFT / NOT APPROVED / NO EXERCISE PERFORMED`

## Immediate actions

1. Make the situation safe and preserve relevant evidence.
2. Contact the designated incident coordinator and ICT support path.
3. Record time, reporter, affected services/data, observed impact and actions taken.
4. Classify operational, security, privacy and care-continuity impact separately.
5. Escalate material events to accountable management and relevant professional privacy/security expertise.

## Assessment

Determine affected systems/data, ongoing threat, impacted persons/services, supplier involvement and recovery needs. Any statutory/regulatory notification decision is reserved for an authorized qualified human and must be time-stamped with its rationale.

## Recovery and learning

Restore safely, verify service integrity, communicate as authorized, record root cause/lessons, create corrective actions and verify closure.

## Evidence needed for operational proof

Approved contacts/escalation matrix, incident log, tabletop/exercise record, real or synthetic test evidence, corrective actions and management review. A written procedure alone proves design, not response effectiveness.
