# Draft AI Use Policy — Synthetic Care Alpha

Status: `DRAFT / NOT APPROVED / PROFESSIONAL CLASSIFICATION REQUIRED`

## Interim rules

1. Use only organizationally approved AI tools/features for work purposes.
2. Do not enter client health data, special-category personal data, credentials or confidential case information into unapproved general-purpose AI services.
3. AI output is assistance, not an autonomous care, employment, legal or security decision.
4. A competent human remains responsible for reviewing material output before use.
5. New AI tools/features/use cases must be registered with purpose, provider, account type, data classes and owner before operational approval.
6. Suspected harmful output, data exposure or uncontrolled AI behaviour is reported through the incident process.
7. Higher-scrutiny uses—including AI touching care records or decisions about people—require professional privacy/security/AI governance review before approval.
8. Personnel receive practical AI-literacy guidance appropriate to their role.

## Implementation evidence needed

Approved version, management approval, communicated rules, AI register, training/awareness record, use-case reviews and exception records. This generated draft alone is not implementation evidence.
