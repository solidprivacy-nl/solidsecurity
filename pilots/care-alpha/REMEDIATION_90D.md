# 90-Day Remediation Plan — Synthetic Care Alpha

Status: draft implementation proposal; accountable customer approval and professional review required.

## Days 0–30 — Stabilize the critical unknowns

**P0: MFA coverage.** Inventory every material authentication path (M365, ECD, privileged MSP, remote access), establish current MFA coverage, remediate gaps or document approved compensating measures. Verification: evidence covers the full relevant user/access population.

**P0: recovery proof.** Define minimum recovery requirements for ECD and identity/productivity services. Execute at least one documented restore/recovery test for critical information. Verification: successful test record including scope, result, exceptions and owner.

**P0: unmanaged AI.** Freeze entry of health/client data into unapproved general-purpose AI tools pending review. Establish initial AI register, approved/prohibited use rules and escalation path. Review the ECD text-suggestion feature separately because it touches care-note data.

**P1: incident readiness.** Refresh incident/datalek/security escalation contacts and create a simple incident log. Schedule a tabletop exercise.

**P1: risk refresh.** Update the information-security risk assessment and convert high risks into owned treatment actions.

## Days 31–60 — Build repeatable governance

- complete system/information asset inventory with owner, criticality and classification;
- formalize joiner/mover/leaver responsibilities and execute first access recertification;
- agree patch/vulnerability RACI and minimum reporting with outsourced ICT;
- complete supplier register and perform structured reviews of the ECD, ICT MSP and Microsoft dependency;
- refresh the personal-data processing register;
- approve concise information-security and AI-use policies;
- close annual awareness coverage gap and add role-specific AI/privacy guidance.

## Days 61–90 — Prove operation

- run and document the incident tabletop;
- repeat/complete restore test where findings remain;
- perform follow-up access review and sample JML evidence;
- review critical supplier actions;
- hold first structured security management review;
- verify closure evidence for P0/P1 findings;
- create recurring calendar for quarterly control/evidence reviews and annual independent assessment.

## Exit state sought

The target is not a statement of legal/certification compliance. The target is a materially stronger evidence state with critical gaps addressed, recurring ownership established, and enough traceability for a professional reviewer to determine next-step assurance readiness.
