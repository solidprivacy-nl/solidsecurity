# Evidence Request — Synthetic Care Alpha

This request is designed to turn declarations into reconstructable evidence. Absence of an item remains visible; it is not treated as a PASS.

## Priority 1 — critical controls

1. Authentication inventory for M365, ECD, VPN/remote support and privileged MSP access, plus current MFA configuration evidence.
2. Most recent ECD backup/restore evidence and one organization-specific restoration test result; documented M365 recovery/retention approach.
3. Current incident contact/escalation list, incident log if any, and evidence from a recent tabletop or exercise.
4. List of AI tools/features actually used, account types, purposes and data classes; vendor information for ECD AI feature.

## Priority 2 — governance and recurring operation

5. Current risk register, treatment owners, due dates and management review record.
6. Joiner/mover/leaver procedure plus a sample of completed cases; current user/access export for first recertification.
7. MSP patch/vulnerability responsibility matrix, SLA/targets and three months of reports.
8. Supplier register with owner, criticality, data access, contract/DPA status, review date and material continuity dependency.
9. Current processing register including new SaaS/AI processing.
10. Awareness/training completion export and role-specific curriculum.

## Priority 3 — proof of management-system operation

11. Formal security accountability decision.
12. Quarterly management security review agenda/minutes template and first completed review.
13. Corrective-action register showing owner, due date, verification criterion and closure evidence.

## Evidence quality checks

For each item record: source, owner, collection date, period/population covered, sensitivity, freshness/expiry, and whether it supports or contradicts the implementation claim.
