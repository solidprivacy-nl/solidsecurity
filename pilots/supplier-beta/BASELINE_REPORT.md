# Supplier Security & Compliance Passport — Synthetic Draft

**Supplier:** Fictieve Softwareleverancier Nimbus B.V.  
**Purpose:** demonstrate questionnaire/passport workflow  
**Status:** proposed evidence-backed claims; not independently assured

## Questionnaire outcome

20 internally authored healthcare-customer security questions were processed.

- 18/20 could reuse existing evidence/claims to some degree;
- 10 produced direct proposed `YES` answers;
- 8 required qualification/partial wording;
- 1 lacked current evidence (penetration-test recency);
- 1 was inherently customer-contract specific;
- 2 questions exposed missing SolidSecurity common-control categories rather than missing supplier evidence.

This is the central commercial finding: a reusable evidence/answer backbone can remove a large share of repeated questionnaire production while preserving the professional review boundary.

## What can be safely communicated

The synthetic supplier can present evidence-linked statements about governance, risk management, identity/access, vulnerability operations, incident response, recovery, supplier/privacy management, awareness and AI governance. Each statement should expose its evidence date/status and whether it was professionally reviewed.

## What must not be communicated yet

- “NIS2 compliant” or “Cbw compliant”;
- “ISO 27001 compliant/certified”;
- an unconditional incident-notification SLA without contract review;
- independent assurance while no independent reviewer/auditor has issued it;
- a current penetration-test claim when the evidence is 18 months old.

## Immediate gaps

1. service-account authentication/access recertification;
2. current independent application testing;
3. wider continuity exercise;
4. one overdue subprocessor review;
5. pending AI-vendor review.

## Product implication

For the Supplier proposition, the highest-value early software may not be a full GRC platform. A structured questionnaire ingester, reusable answer/provenance store, evidence-expiry engine and review queue could deliver substantial customer value much earlier.
