# SolidSecurity

AI-first managed compliance for small healthcare organizations and SMEs that need to demonstrate structured, secure and privacy-conscious operations under growing regulatory and supply-chain pressure.

## Current stage

`CONCEPT / OPERATING MODEL DESIGN`

The project is deliberately starting with a small governance and workflow backbone. Product implementation is not yet authorized; the concept, positioning, operating model, architecture, selective open-source reuse and roadmap are first being worked through and discussed.

## Target problem

SolidSecurity is aimed primarily at organizations that are too small for a full internal security/compliance function but increasingly need to demonstrate control in areas such as:

- Dutch Cybersecurity Act / NIS2 and NIS2-driven supply-chain requirements;
- NEN 7510 and healthcare information security;
- ISO 27001 readiness and related assurance requirements;
- GDPR/privacy governance;
- AI Act / responsible AI governance.

## Delivery principle

AI is leverage, not the source of trust.

AI may perform repeatable analysis, drafting, mapping, evidence organization and workflow support. Material professional judgment, exceptions, assurance and final legal/certification conclusions remain subject to qualified human review.
