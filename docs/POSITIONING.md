# Positioning Doctrine

## Primary customer-facing position

**Aantoonbaar in control. Zonder eigen complianceafdeling.**

SolidSecurity manages the recurring work required to demonstrate structured security, privacy and responsible AI governance for small organizations.

## Secondary promise

**Eén keer organiseren. Meervoudig aantonen. Continu onderhouden.**

The same controls and evidence are reused across relevant regulatory, sector and assurance contexts.

## AI messaging rule

Do not lead with “AI compliance”. Lead with the business outcome and trust model.

Preferred sequence:

1. customer outcome — demonstrably in control;
2. scope — security, privacy and AI governance;
3. service model — managed, recurring, no internal department required;
4. credibility — evidence and professional review;
5. mechanism — AI/automation removes repetitive work and therefore improves speed and economics.

## Credibility claims

Safe claims:

- “AI and automation reduce repetitive compliance work.”
- “Material conclusions are professionally reviewed.”
- “One control can support multiple frameworks.”
- “We maintain evidence and actions continuously rather than producing only a one-off report.”
- “We prepare organizations for independent audits; certification remains with the certification body.”

Claims to avoid:

- “100% compliant.”
- “AI guarantees NIS2/ISO/NEN compliance.”
- “Automatic certification.”
- “Replace your DPO/CISO/auditor completely.”
- “Cheapest in the market.”

## Segment messaging

### Small healthcare

**NEN 7510, privacy, cyberweerbaarheid en AI-governance beheersbaar maken — zonder een eigen securityteam op te bouwen.**

### SME suppliers

**Bewijs richting klanten en ketenpartners dat uw organisatie veilig en beheerst werkt, zonder iedere vragenlijst opnieuw uit te vinden.**

### Certification readiness

**Van losse documenten naar een aantoonbaar werkend managementsysteem dat klaar is voor onafhankelijke audit.**

## “Better, faster, cheaper” interpretation

Do not use this as an unsupported headline. Prove it operationally:

- **better** = consistent control model, traceability, evidence state and review;
- **faster** = reuse and AI-assisted production;
- **cheaper** = fewer professional hours spent on repetitive administration;
- **credible** = human accountability remains at material decision points.
