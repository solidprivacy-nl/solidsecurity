# Managed Compliance Operating Model

## 1. Service model

SolidSecurity is a **managed operating system for compliance work**. It combines a standardized control model with client-specific implementation/evidence and recurring professional review.

The customer buys maintained assurance capability, not raw consulting hours and not merely access to software.

## 2. Core production loop

```text
FACTS
  ↓
SCOPE
  ↓
REQUIREMENTS
  ↓
COMMON CONTROLS
  ↓
CLIENT IMPLEMENTATION CLAIMS
  ↓
EVIDENCE
  ↓
ASSESSMENT
  ↓
FINDINGS / ACTIONS
  ↓
PROFESSIONAL REVIEW
  ↓
REPORT / STATUS
  ↓
RECURRING REASSESSMENT
```

## 3. Roles

### Client accountable owner

Provides facts, approves internal policies/decisions and remains accountable for the organization's real implementation.

### SolidSecurity implementation professional

Guides onboarding, validates facts, resolves AI uncertainty, designs/remediates controls and owns operational client delivery.

### AI operator/agent

Performs bounded extraction, drafting, mapping, comparison, classification, evidence organization and recommendation tasks. It is an attributed contributor, never a hidden final authority.

### Professional reviewer

Reviews material assessments, evidence sufficiency and exceptions. The reviewer may approve, reject, request additional evidence or escalate.

### Independent auditor/certification body

External where a formal independent certification/assurance claim is required. SolidSecurity prepares but does not impersonate this role.

## 4. Onboarding outputs

A baseline engagement should produce:

- confirmed organization/scope facts;
- applicable service/framework scope;
- initial asset/system/supplier/process inventory at the level required for the service;
- control applicability set;
- implementation/evidence state;
- findings and risks;
- prioritized 90-day remediation plan;
- required policies/registers/procedures;
- professional review record;
- management-readable baseline report;
- recurring review calendar.

## 5. Recurring service cadence

The default managed cycle is exception-driven, not document-driven.

### Continuous/ongoing

- intake of changed facts or documents;
- evidence expiry tracking;
- supplier/AI-use updates;
- action tracking;
- regulatory/source change triage.

### Monthly or service-tier cadence

- review open high-priority findings/actions;
- detect stale evidence/controls;
- produce concise management status;
- review material changes.

### Quarterly

- structured control/evidence refresh;
- risk and supplier review where applicable;
- professional verification sample/priority review;
- roadmap reprioritization.

### Annually

- full management-system review;
- policy lifecycle review;
- scope/applicability refresh;
- audit-readiness refresh.

Cadence is adjusted to the client/service tier and applicable requirements.

## 6. Output states

SolidSecurity should not reduce the client to one synthetic “compliance percentage”. It should show at least:

- applicable controls;
- designed controls;
- implemented claims;
- evidenced controls;
- professionally verified controls;
- independently assured controls;
- unresolved findings/actions;
- expired/stale evidence;
- material exceptions.

## 7. Unit-economics objective

AI should remove low-value professional production time while **increasing** the proportion of time spent on:

- fact validation;
- difficult judgment;
- remediation design;
- evidence challenge;
- management guidance;
- exception handling.

The target metric is not “AI automation percentage” by itself, but **professional minutes per verified control/outcome** while maintaining review quality.
