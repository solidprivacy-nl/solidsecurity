# Service Deliverable Taxonomy V1

Status: `PRODUCT DESIGN / PRICING NOT COMMITTED`

The same underlying controls/evidence/review model supports distinct outcomes. Packages should differ mainly in scope, cadence and professional assurance effort, not by creating separate compliance databases.

## 1. SolidSecurity Baseline

Purpose: establish a reconstructable current state and practical priority plan.

Typical outputs:

- organization/scope fact pack;
- applicable-framework questions and pending professional scope decisions;
- control implementation claims;
- evidence catalogue and evidence request;
- proposed + included professional-reviewed assessment according to contract;
- prioritized findings;
- 60/90-day remediation plan;
- baseline management report.

Claim boundary: **current-state/readiness assessment**, not certification or universal compliance verdict.

## 2. SolidSecurity Care Managed

Purpose: keep a small care organization's information security, privacy and AI-governance evidence systematically current.

Typical recurring outputs:

- NEN 7510-oriented care control view without reproducing protected norm content;
- Cbw/NIS2 direct/supply-chain readiness where applicable;
- risk/action register maintenance;
- evidence expiry/review cycle;
- supplier review cycle;
- privacy processing/AI-use governance support;
- management review pack;
- professional exception/materiality review;
- annual independent-assessment coordination where required.

Claim boundary: **managed, evidence-linked control state with explicit review status**.

## 3. SolidSecurity Supplier Passport

Purpose: help smaller B2B suppliers demonstrate security/privacy governance to customers without recreating every questionnaire from scratch.

Typical outputs:

- approved assertion library;
- evidence-linked Security & Compliance Passport;
- questionnaire ingestion/mapping;
- proposed reusable answers;
- professional review of qualified/contract-specific/material statements;
- evidence expiry and refresh requests;
- remediation actions blocking stronger customer assertions.

Claim boundary: passport is **evidence-linked supplier assurance information**, not independent audit or certification unless separately labelled with actual evidence.

## 4. SolidSecurity Audit Ready

Purpose: organize an organization toward an external ISO 27001/NEN 7510 or other agreed assessment/certification process.

Typical outputs:

- scope/readiness assessment;
- management-system artifacts;
- risk/control/evidence traceability;
- Statement-of-Applicability/readiness support where legally/licensing permissible;
- internal review/findings/corrective actions;
- management review preparation;
- external-audit evidence pack and finding workflow.

Claim boundary: **audit/certification readiness support**. The external auditor/certification body makes the independent formal decision.

## Common commercial principle

AI leverage should reduce repetitive production cost while professional time is concentrated on material scope, evidence sufficiency, exceptions, risk and customer-facing assurance. Lower cost is an operating-model consequence, not permission to reduce review quality.
