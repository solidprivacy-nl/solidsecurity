# Contract, DPA and Subprocessor Requirements V1

Real client data may not enter the platform until the commercial/data-processing chain is documented.

## Client agreement must define

- service scope and explicit non-certification/non-legal-verdict boundaries;
- controller/processor roles where applicable;
- categories of client data/evidence expected;
- instructions and permitted processing purposes;
- retention/offboarding arrangement;
- incident/security notification channel;
- approved professional-assurance wording;
- client responsibilities for fact/evidence accuracy.

## Subprocessor/provider register

For each provider handling client-plane data record:

- legal entity/provider;
- service purpose;
- data categories;
- hosting/processing regions;
- subprocessors where relevant;
- DPA/contract reference;
- retention/no-training terms for AI providers;
- security/assurance evidence;
- transfer mechanism where relevant;
- review date and owner.

## Change management

New model/storage/observability/support providers that can access client data require review before activation. Material subprocessor changes are handled according to contract/DPA obligations.

## Provider minimization

Do not add a SaaS provider merely because integration is convenient. Each provider expands the trust/subprocessor surface and must solve a demonstrated workflow need.
