# Client Lifecycle Workflow

## Stage 0 — Qualification

Determine whether the organization matches the service boundary.

Capture only minimum pre-contract facts:

- sector and approximate size;
- services/criticality;
- certification ambitions;
- direct Cbw/NIS2 exposure uncertainty;
- major customer/chain requirements;
- health/personal-data context;
- AI use maturity;
- urgency and known incidents/audit deadlines.

Output: service fit + proposed scope. No compliance verdict.

## Stage 1 — Contract and data boundary

Before real documents/evidence:

- approved engagement scope;
- DPA/processor/subprocessor position as applicable;
- approved secure workspace;
- retention/deletion rules;
- named accountable client owner;
- professional review class.

No client material enters the public project repository.

## Stage 2 — Fact intake

Client supplies structured facts and existing documents.

AI may:

- inventory files;
- extract candidate systems, suppliers, processes, policies and AI uses;
- identify missing answers.

Professional/client validates material extracted facts.

Output: `FACT_BASELINE` with provenance and uncertainty.

## Stage 3 — Scope and applicability

Determine which service packs/frameworks/requirements are relevant.

AI may propose; qualified human approves material legal/regulatory applicability.

Output: approved scope and applicable control set.

## Stage 4 — Current-state assessment

For each applicable SolidSecurity control:

1. record implementation claim;
2. link available evidence;
3. identify contradiction/missing evidence;
4. propose Proof Ladder state;
5. create findings/actions.

## Stage 5 — Remediation design

Prioritize by risk, regulatory urgency, customer value and implementation effort.

Output: 30/60/90-day plan with owners and verification criteria.

AI may draft policies/procedures; organizational owner must approve and actual implementation must be separately evidenced.

## Stage 6 — Professional baseline review

Reviewer checks material applicability, evidence sufficiency, high-risk findings and report language.

Output: approved baseline status with explicit unknowns/limitations.

## Stage 7 — Management report

Customer sees:

- scope;
- proof states;
- high-priority risks/findings;
- overdue/missing evidence;
- actions and owners;
- framework readiness views;
- review date and limitations.

Avoid misleading single “compliance percentage” as the only status signal.

## Stage 8 — Managed recurring cycle

- new/change facts;
- evidence refresh;
- action closure verification;
- supplier and AI-use changes;
- source/regulatory change triage;
- periodic professional review;
- management update.

## Stage 9 — Audit/certification support where relevant

Prepare evidence pack, SoA/required records, internal review findings and corrective actions. External certification/audit remains independent.

## Stage 10 — Offboarding

Provide export, revoke access, complete retention/deletion obligations and preserve only records legally/contractually justified.
