# SolidSecurity Care Workflow V1

## Objective

Provide a manageable security/privacy/AI governance operating model for small healthcare organizations without requiring an internal security department.

## Baseline scope candidates

- NEN 7510-aligned information-security management;
- Cbw/NIS2 readiness or direct obligations where applicable;
- GDPR security/privacy governance interfaces;
- AI governance baseline;
- selected ISO 27001 readiness views where commercially useful.

Applicability is client-specific and professionally reviewed.

## Care onboarding focus

### Organization

- legal entities and care services;
- locations and critical care processes;
- accountable management and ICT/security ownership.

### Information and systems

- ECD/EPD or care information systems;
- Microsoft 365/identity environment;
- telecare/nurse-call/care-technology platforms where in scope;
- mobile devices/endpoints;
- backups and continuity dependencies.

### Suppliers

- managed ICT/provider;
- ECD/health software vendors;
- hosting/cloud providers;
- care-technology suppliers;
- processors/subprocessors relevant to health information.

### Security/privacy operations

- access/MFA;
- joiner/mover/leaver;
- patching/vulnerability responsibilities;
- backup/restore testing;
- incident and data-breach handling;
- continuity;
- supplier agreements/reviews;
- awareness/training.

### AI

- AI tools actually used by staff;
- purpose and data entered;
- provider/account type;
- approved/prohibited use;
- AI literacy;
- human oversight;
- escalation for potentially high-risk use cases.

## First 90 days example

### Days 0–30 — Know and stabilize

- confirm scope and ownership;
- build minimum system/supplier/AI inventories;
- identify immediate high-risk gaps;
- establish incident contacts;
- check access/MFA/backups at evidence level available to the engagement;
- agree priority policies and controls.

### Days 31–60 — Implement core governance

- approve security/privacy/AI policies;
- implement priority access/supplier/incident/continuity actions;
- establish evidence collection routine;
- train accountable staff;
- resolve high-priority missing evidence.

### Days 61–90 — Verify and operationalize

- test selected controls such as restore/incident/access review;
- professional review of evidence;
- close or time-bound remaining findings;
- management review;
- set recurring cadence.

## Customer-facing result

Not “you are 100% NEN/NIS2 compliant”, but an evidence-backed state such as:

- applicable common controls: 42;
- designed: 40;
- implemented: 35;
- evidenced: 29;
- professionally verified: 24;
- open high-priority findings: 3;
- next review/actions: explicit.

Numbers above are illustrative only.
