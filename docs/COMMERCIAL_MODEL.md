# Commercial Model Hypotheses

## Principle

Sell maintained outcomes and assurance capability, not AI tokens or bundles of consultant hours.

Pricing should reflect complexity, review intensity and evidence burden more than employee count alone.

## Initial packages

### SolidSecurity Care Essential

Indicative: **EUR 495–695/month** plus onboarding.

Designed for very small care providers with a bounded scope and lower review cadence.

Possible scope:

- baseline control register;
- core policy/register maintenance;
- NEN 7510/Cbw-readiness mappings relevant to scope;
- privacy/security baseline;
- AI governance baseline;
- action tracking;
- periodic professional review.

### SolidSecurity Care Assured

Indicative: **EUR 895–1,295/month** plus onboarding.

Adds higher professional-review cadence, supplier/risk cycles, stronger evidence maintenance and audit preparation.

### SolidSecurity Supplier

Indicative: **EUR 495–895/month** depending on questionnaire/customer burden.

Core outcome: maintained compliance/security passport and reuse of approved answers/evidence across customer questionnaires.

### Higher-regulation / complex scope

Indicative starting point: **~EUR 1,500/month**, then complexity-based.

### Baseline/onboarding

Indicative: **EUR 1,500–3,000** for small organizations; more where scope/data complexity is materially higher.

### Audit Ready project

Indicative: **EUR 3,500–7,500+** depending on starting maturity and certification scope.

SolidSecurity prepares the management system/evidence; independent certification remains external.

## Complexity drivers

- number of legal entities/scopes;
- number and criticality of systems/suppliers;
- applicable frameworks/regulatory exposure;
- personal/health data sensitivity;
- desired assurance/review cadence;
- certification ambitions;
- questionnaire/tender volume;
- number/complexity of AI use cases;
- remediation backlog.

## Unit economics

Do not target maximum automation at the cost of assurance. Optimize:

`gross margin + professional review quality + customer price advantage`.

Useful internal metrics:

- professional hours per client/month;
- professional minutes per verified control;
- AI-draft acceptance/edit rate;
- evidence reuse ratio;
- questionnaire answer reuse ratio;
- time from evidence request to verified state;
- recurring revenue per professional FTE;
- remediation conversion/closure rate.

## Pricing validation rule

These price points remain hypotheses until Phase 1/2 workload measurement. Record actual effort by workflow step before fixing packages publicly.
