# ADR 0003 — AI suggests; humans assure

- Status: Accepted for Foundation V1
- Date: 2026-08-15

## Context

AI can materially reduce compliance production effort but can also hallucinate, overstate evidence and blur accountability.

## Decision

AI is an attributed contributor operating through bounded authority classes. It may create drafts, proposed mappings and proposed assessments. Material proof-level promotion, risk acceptance, legal/regulatory conclusions and independent assurance require authorized human or external authority.

## Consequences

The product can use aggressive automation without making AI the source of trust. Review workflow, provenance and state-transition controls are architectural features, not optional process guidance.
