# ADR 0002 — Common control backbone

- Status: Accepted for Foundation V1
- Date: 2026-08-15

## Context

Customers face overlapping obligations across Cbw/NIS2, NEN 7510, ISO 27001, GDPR, AI governance and customer assurance requests. Separate checklists duplicate implementation and evidence work.

## Decision

SolidSecurity will maintain framework-neutral internally authored controls and map multiple external requirements to those controls.

Requirements, controls, client implementation claims and evidence remain distinct objects.

## Consequences

Mappings become a high-value maintained asset. Versioning and professional review are required. Protected standard text must not be copied merely to simplify mapping.
